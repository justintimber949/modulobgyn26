# Koriokarsinoma (Choriocarcinoma) & Neoplasia Trofoblas Gestasional (GTN)

## Cerita Pembuka

Ny. Sari, 28 tahun, G1P0A1, riwayat **mola hidatidosa 6 bulan lalu (kuretase, histopat: mola komplit)**. Kontrol β-hCG pasca kuretase: **minggu 1: 150.000, minggu 2: 85.000, minggu 3: 60.000, minggu 4: 55.000 (plat), minggu 8: 70.000 (naik)**. Keluhan: **spotting pervaginam, sesak napas seitan 2 minggu**. Pemeriksaan: **USG: Uterus Diperbesar, Endometrium Tebal, Tidak Ada Janin, Ovarium: Theca Lutein Cyst Bilateral 6 cm**. CXR: **Multiple Nodula Paru Kanan Kiri**. CT: **Metastasis Paru, Hati, Otak**. β-hCG: **245.000 mIU/mL**. FIGO 2000 Stage III:7 (Metastasis Paru + Hati + Otak, Skor WHO 7 → High Risk). Kemoterapi: **EMA-CO (Etoposide, Methotrexate, Actinomycin-D / Cyclophosphamide, Vincristine) × 6 Siklus**. β-hCG Normalisasi Siklus 4. Lanjut 2 Siklus Konsolidasi. 12 Bulan Pasca Normal: NED, β-hCG Normal, Menstruasi Kembali, Hamil Spontan 18 Bulan Nanti, Lahir Sehat.

## Definisi & Klasifikasi (WHO / FIGO)

**Gestational Trophoblastic Neoplasia (GTN)** = Neoplasia Maligna Dari **Trofoblas (Sitotrofoblas + Sinkiotrofoblas)** Pasca Kehamilan (Mola, Abortus, Kehamilan Normal, Kehamilan Ektopik).

| Entitas GTN                                   | Definisi                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Mola Invasif (Invasive Mole)**              | **Trofoblas Invasif Ke Miometrium** (Tidak Metastasis Jauh) — Sering Post-Mola Komplit                                               |
| **Koriokarsinoma (Choriocarcinoma)**          | **Malignan Penuh: Sitotrofoblas + Sinkiotrofoblas Tanpa Klorionik Vili (Tidak Ada Vili)** — Bisa Post-Mola, Abortus, Partus, Ektopik |
| **Placental Site Trophoblastic Tumor (PSTT)** | **Jarang, Berasal Dari Sitotrofoblas Intermediate (Implantasi) — β-hCG Rendah, hPL Tinggi, Perilaku Lambat**                         |
| **Epithelioid Trophoblastic Tumor (ETT)**     | **Sangat Jarang, Berasal Dari Sitotrofoblas Klorionik — β-hCG Rendah, hPL Tinggi**                                                   |

> **Kebanyakan GTN Klinis = Mola Invasif ATAU Koriokarsinoma (Sulit Beda Tanpa Histopat) — Tatalaksana SAMA (Kemoterapi Berdasarkan Risk Score).**

## Etiologi & Faktor Risiko

| Kehamilan Sebelumnya          | Risiko GTN                      |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Mola Hidatidosa Komplit**   | **15-20% (Paling Tinggi)**      |
| **Mola Parsial**              | **0,5-1%**                      |
| **Abortus Spontan / Induksi** | **~ 1:500-1000**                |
| **Kehamilan Normal (Term)**   | **~ 1:20.000-40.000**           |
| **Kehamilan Ektopik**         | **~ 1:10.000**                  |
| **Usia > 40 Th / < 15 Th**    | **Risiko ↑**                    |
| **Riwayat GTN Sebelumnya**    | **Risiko ↑↑ (Recurrence Risk)** |

## Patogenesis

- **Mola Komplit (46,XX Diploid, Andesogenetik: 2 Spermatozoa Fertilize Oosit Tanpa Nukleus) → Proliferasi Trofoblas Masif → Invasif/Metastasis**
- **Koriokarsinoma: Bisa Dari Mola, Abortus, Term, Ektopik — **Monoklonal, Agresif, Angiogenik Tinggi (VEGF ↑), Metastasis Hematogen Cepat (Paru, Otak, Hati, Ginjal, Vagina, Kulit)\*\*

## Gejala & Tanda (Bergantung Situs Metastasis)

| Gejala                                                                               | Frekuensi             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| **PMB / Bleeding Per Vaginam (Post-Mola/Abortus/Partus) — **Palting\*\*              | **> 90%**             |
| **β-hCG Tinggi / Tidak Turun / Naik Lagi (Post-Kehamilan)**                          | **100% (Diagnostik)** |
| **Asites / Massa Pelvis / Uterus Diperbesar**                                        | 50%                   |
| **Gejala Metastasis Paru: Batuk, Sesak, Hemoptisis, Pleural Effusion**               | 30%                   |
| **Gejala Metastasis Otak: Sakit Kepala, Nyeri Saraf Kranial, Seizure, Hemiparesis**  | 10-15%                |
| **Gejala Metastasis Hati: Nyeri Hipokondrium Kanan, Hepatomegali, Fungsi Hati Naik** | 10%                   |
| **Gejala Metastasis Vagina: Nodula Biru-Ungu (Blueberry) Di Vagina/Serviks**         | 5-10% (Tanda Khusus)  |
| **Teorombositopenia / Koagulopati (DIC) — Jarang**                                   | < 5%                  |

## Diagnosis & Workup

### 1. β-hCG Kuantitatif (Serum) — **Gold Standard Diagnosis & Monitoring**

| Kriteria Diagnosis GTN (FIGO 2000 / WHO) — **Salah Satu Cukup**                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. β-hCG Platau: 4× Pengukuran Mingguan Tidak Turun ≥ 10% (Contoh: 50.000 → 48.000 → 47.000 → 46.500)** |
| **2. β-hCG Naik: 3× Pengukuran Mingguan Naik ≥ 10% (Contoh: 10.000 → 11.500 → 13.000)**                   |
| **3. β-hCG Persisten: > 6 Bulan Pasca Evakuasi Mola / Abortus / Partus**                                  |
| **4. Histopatologi: Koriokarsinoma / Mola Invasif / PSTT / ETT**                                          |
| **5. Metastasis Klinis/Radiologis + β-hCG Tinggi**                                                        |

> **β-hCG Normalisasi: < 5 mIU/mL (Atau < 2 mIU/mL Assay Modern) — **Kriteria Remisi\*\*."

### 2. USG Transvaginal

- **Uterus Diperbesar, Endometrium Tebal Heterogen, Vaskularitas Tinggi (Color Doppler)**
- **Ovarium: Theca Lutein Cyst (Bilateral, Multilokuler, > 6 cm) — β-hCG Tinggi Stimulasi — **Bisa Ruptur/Torsi, Biasanya Regresi Spontan Pasca β-hCG Normal\*\*"
- \*\*Rule Out: Retensi Jaringan Kehamilan (RPOC) — β-hCG Turun Lambat Tapi Menurun, USG: Materi Ekogenik Di Rongga Uterus"

### 3. Staging Imaging (WAJIB Jika GTN Terkonfirmasi)

| Modality                            | Situs                                                               | Indikasi                                                      |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **CXR (Thorax Photo) / CT Thoraks** | **Paru (Paling Sering), Pleura, Mediastinum**                       | **WAJIB Semua GTN**                                           |
| **CT Abdomen/Pelvis / MRI**         | **Hati, Ginjal, Adrenal, Limfa Paraaortik, Ovarium, Uterus Detail** | **High Risk / Stage III-IV / Gejala Abdominal**               |
| **MRI Otak / CT Head**              | **Otak (Metastasis Hematogen Cepat)**                               | **High Risk (Skor WHO ≥ 7) / Gejala Saraf / β-hCG > 100.000** |
| **PET-CT (FDG)**                    | **Whole Body (Lebih Sensitif Limfa & Metastasis Kecil)**            | **Opsi Jika Tersedia / Recurrence / Persisten"**              |

### 4. FIGO 2000 Staging + WHO Risk Score (Menentukan Kemoterapi)

#### **Stage (Anatomic)**

| Stage   | Definisi                                                                                                  |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **I**   | **Kondisi Terbatas Uterus**                                                                               |
| **II**  | **Ekstensi Ke Struktur Genital Pelvik (Vagina, Serviks Parametrium, Tuba, Ovarium, Ligamen Uterosakral)** |
| **III** | **Metastasis Paru (Dengan Atau Tanpa Genital Pelvik)**                                                    |
| **IV**  | **Metastasis Jauh Lain (Hati, Otak, Ginjal, GI, Kulit, Limfa Jauh)**                                      |

#### **WHO Prognostic Scoring System (Skor 0-25)**

| Faktor                                       | Skor 0 | Skor 1        | Skor 2      | Skor 4                               |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Usia**                                     | < 40   | ≥ 40          | -           | -                                    |
| **Kehamilan Sebelumnya**                     | Mola   | Abortus       | Term        | -                                    |
| **Interval (Bulan) Dari Kehamilan Terakhir** | < 4    | 4-6           | 7-12        | > 12                                 |
| **β-hCG (mIU/mL)**                           | < 10³  | 10³-10⁴       | 10⁴-10⁵     | > 10⁵                                |
| **Ukuran Tumor Terbesar (cm)**               | < 3    | 3-5           | > 5         | -                                    |
| **Situs Metastasis**                         | Paru   | Vagina/Pelvis | Hati/Ginjal | Otak                                 |
| **Jumlah Metastasis**                        | 0      | 1-4           | 5-8         | > 8                                  |
| **Kemoterapi Gagal Sebelumnya**              | Tidak  | -             | -           | Ya (Single Agent) / Ya (Multi-Agent) |

| **Total Skor** | **Kategori Risiko** | **Terapi**                                       |
| -------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **≤ 6**        | **Low Risk**        | **Single Agent (MTX / Act-D)**                   |
| **≥ 7**        | **High Risk**       | **Multi-Agent (EMA-CO / EP-EMA / FAEV / TP/TE)** |

> **FIGO Stage + Risk Score = Contoh: Stage III:7 (Stage III, Skor 7 = High Risk)"**

## Tata Laksana

### A. Low Risk (Skor ≤ 6) — **Single Agent Chemotherapy**

| Regimen                      | Dosis & Jadwal                                                                                                                                                                            | Respons Rate |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| **Methotrexate (MTX) IM/IV** | **50 mg/m² IM/IV Hari 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 (1 Minggu Istirahat) — **8 Hari Siklus** ATAU **300 mg/m² IV Hari 1 + Leukovorin 15 mg PO/IV 24, 36, 48, 60 Jam Pasca MTX (High-Dose MTX)\*\* | **85-95%**   |
| **Actinomycin-D (Act-D) IV** | **1,25 mg/m² IV Hari 1-5 (5 Hari Berturut) Tiap 14 Hari** ATAU **0,5 mg/m² IV Hari 1 Tiap 14 Hari (Low-Dose Pulse)**                                                                      | **85-95%**   |

> **Pilih MTX Jika Fungsi Hati Normal, Act-D Jika Fungsi Hati Gangguan / MTX Gagal / Alergi — **Ganti Obat Jika Resisten (β-hCG Naik/Platou 3 Siklus)\*\*."

### B. High Risk (Skor ≥ 7) — **Multi-Agent Chemotherapy**

| Regimen (Standar Global)                                                                  | Dosis & Jadwal (21 Hari/Siklus)                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Catatan                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **EMA-CO (Etoposide, Methotrexate, Actinomycin-D / Cyclophosphamide, Vincristine)**       | **Hari 1-2: Etoposide 100 mg/m² IV + MTX 300 mg/m² IV + Leukovorin 15 mg 24,36,48,60 Jam + Act-D 0,5 mg/m² IV — **Hari 8-9: Cyclophosphamide 600 mg/m² IV + Vincristine 1 mg/m² IV (Max 2 mg) — **Mesna, Hidrasi, G-CSF Dukungan** | **First-Line Gold Standard (WHO/FIGO/NCCN) — Respons 85-95%, OS > 90%** |
| **EP-EMA (Etoposide, Cisplatin / Etoposide, Methotrexate, Act-D)**                        | **Alternatif Jika EMA-CO Gagal / Neurotoksisitas Vincristine / Nefrotoksisitas Cisplatin Diperlukan**                                                                                                                              | \*\*Second-Line / Salvage"                                              |
| **FAEV (5-FU, Act-D, Etoposide, Vincristine) / TP/TE (Paclitaxel/Carboplatin/Etoposide)** | \*\*Salvage / Recurrence / Resisten Platinum"                                                                                                                                                                                      | \*\*Third-Line+"                                                        |

> **Siklus: **Minimum 3 Siklus PASCA β-hCG Normal (Normalisasi) — **Total Biasanya 6-8 Siklus First-Line" — **Konsolidasi Penting Mencegah Recurrence!"\*\*

### C. Operasi (Adjuvant / Paliatif)

| Indikasi                                                                                                                                       | Prosedur                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Uterus: Blooding Masif / Infeksi / Perforasi / Resisten Kemo (β-hCG Persisten > 4-6 Siklus Multi-Agent) / Selesai Keturunan (Usia > 40 Th)** | **Histerektomi Total (Bisa BSO Jika Ovarium Terlibat / Theca Lutein Cyst Komplikasi) — **Salpingektomi Bilateral Direkomendasikan (Pencegahan GTN Baru / Ovarium Cancer)"\*\* |
| **Metastasis Tunggal/Resectable (Paru, Hati, Otak, Vagina)**                                                                                   | \*\*Reseksi Metastasis (Thorakotomi/VATS, Hepatektomi, Kraniotomi, Vaginektomi Parsial) + Kemo Adjuvant"                                                                      |
| **Theca Lutein Cyst Ovarium > 10 cm / Torsi / Ruptur**                                                                                         | **Oophorektomi / Cistektomi (Jika Komplikasi Akut) — **Biasanya Regresi Spontan Pasca Kemo, Hindari Oophorektomi Rutin\*\*"                                                   |

### D. Dukungan & Monitoring

- **G-CSF (Filgrastim/Pegfilgrastim) Profaktik Siklus Multi-Agent (EMA-CO) — Neutropenia Febril Risiko Tinggi**
- **Anti-Emetik (5-HT3 Antagonis + Deksametason + NK1 Antagonis Aprepitant) — Cisplatin/Etoposide Emetogenik Tinggi**
- **Hidrasi + Mesna (Uroproteksi Cyclophosphamide/Ifosfamid)**
- **Transfusi Darah/Trombosit Jika Anemia/Trombositopenia Berat**
- **Kontraksi Hamil WAJIB Selama Kemo & 12 Bulan Pasca Normalisasi β-hCG (IUD/Kondom/Implan/COC Jika Normalisasi) — **Teratogenik Tinggi\*\*"

## Follow-up & Remisi

| Fase                                               | Monitoring                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Selama Kemo**                                    | **β-hCG Mingguan (Sebelum Tiap Siklus) + CBC + Liver/Kidney + CXR (Bulan) — **Respon: β-hCG Turun ≥ 10%/Minggu = Baik\*\*                                                          |
| **Pasca Normalisasi (Remisi)**                     | **β-hCG Mingguan × 4 Minggu → 2 Mingguan × 3 Bulan → Bulanan × 12 Bulan → 3 Bulanan × 12 Bulan → 6 Bulanan × 5 Tahun — **Total 5 Tahun (Risiko Recurrence Tertinggi 12-24 Bln)\*\* |
| **Kontraksi**                                      | **WAJIB 12 Bulan Pasca Normalisasi β-hCG — **IUD (Kopper/Hormonal) / Implan / Kondom — **COC Bisa Setelah Normalisasi (Estrogen Tidak Mempengaruhi GTN)**"                         |
| **Kehamilan Berikutnya**                           | **Risiko Recurrence Mola/GTN ↑ (1-2%) — **USG Dini (6-8 Mg) + β-hCG Kuantitatif Serum + Histopat Plakenta Post-Partus\*\*                                                          |
| **Recurrence (β-hCG Naik Lagi Pasca Normalisasi)** | \*\*Restaging (Imaging) + Multi-Agent Kemo (EMA-CO Jika Belum / EP-EMA / FAEV / TP-TE / Klinis Trial) ± Operasi Reseksi Metastasis"                                                |

## Prognosis

| Kategori                                                             | 5-Year OS  | Fertilitas Pasca                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Low Risk (Single Agent)**                                          | **> 99%**  | **Normal (80-90% Hamil Spontan)**                                                                                                     |
| **High Risk (Multi-Agent EMA-CO)**                                   | **85-95%** | **Normal (70-80% Hamil Spontan, Bisa IVF Jika Dibutuhkan)**                                                                           |
| **Very High Risk (Skor > 12 / Otak / Hati Metastasis / Kemo Gagal)** | **60-80%** | **Bisa Terganggu (Premature Ovarian Insufficiency) — **Fertility Preservation (Oocyte/Embryo Freezing) SEBELUM KEMO Jika Mungkin\*\*" |

## Ringkasan untuk Dokter Umum

1. **GTN (Koriokarsinoma/Mola Invasif) = Neoplasia Trofoblas Pasca Kehamilan — **β-hCG = Marker Tumor Sempurna (Diagnosis, Monitoring, Recurrence)\*\*"
2. **Diagnosis GTN: **β-hCG Platou/Naik/Persisten > 6 Bln / Histopat / Metastasis + β-hCG Tinggi** — **TIDAK Perlu Biopsi Uterus (Risiko Blooding/Seeding) — Diagnosis Klinis + β-hCG Cukup!"\*\*
3. **Staging: **FIGO Stage (I-IV) + WHO Risk Score (0-25) → Low Risk (≤ 6) vs High Risk (≥ 7)\*\*"
4. **Low Risk: **Single Agent MTX atau Act-D (Kurang Toxik, Outpatient Bisa) — **High Risk: **EMA-CO (Gold Standard) — **Minimum 3 Siklus Pasca Normalisasi β-hCG (Total 6-8 Siklus)**"
5. **Imaging: **CXR/CT Thoraks WAJIB Semua, CT Abdomen/Pelvis + MRI Otak High Risk\*\*"
6. **Operasi: **Histerektomi Jika Blooding/Infeksi/Resisten/Selesai Keturunan — **Ovarium Dipertahankan (Kecuali Komplikasi) — **Salpingektomi Bilateral Direkomendasikan**"**
7. **Follow-up: **β-hCG Ketat 5 Tahun (Mingguan → Bulanan → 6 Bulanan) — **Kontraksi 12 Bln Pasca Normalisasi**"
8. **Kehamilan Lalu: **USG Dini + β-hCG + Histopat Plakenta — **Risiko Mola/GTN Recurrence 1-2%**""
9. **Prognosis Sangat Baik (Low Risk ~100%, High Risk ~90%) — **Fertilitas Terjaga Mayoritas\*\*""
