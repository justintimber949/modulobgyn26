# Sindrom Polikistik Ovarium (PCOS)

## Cerita Pembuka

Ny. Rina, 24 tahun, G0, keluhan: **tidak haid 3-4 bln (oligomenorea), jerawat berat, rambut lebat wajah (hirsutisme), BB naik 15 kg dalam 2 th (BMI 32)**. USG TVS: **Ovarium Bilateral Diperbesar (Volume 14 & 12 mL), ≥ 12 Folikel 2-9 mm Masing-Masing (String of Pearls), Stroma Hiperekoik**. Lab: **Testosteron Total 85 ng/dL (Tinggi), Free T Tinggi, LH 18, FSH 6 (Ratio LH:FSH > 2), AMH 8 ng/mL (Tinggi), Insulin Puasa 18 μIU/mL (Tinggi), HOMA-IR 3,8 (Insulin Resistensi), Glukosa Puasa 95, HbA1c 5,6%, Lipid: TG 180, HDL 38, LDL 130**. Diagnosis: **PCOS (Rotterdam: Oligoanovulasi + Hiperandrogenisme Klinis/Biokimia + Ovarium Polikistik USG) + Obesitas + Insulin Resistensi + Dislipidemia**. Manajemen: **Lifestyle (Diet Low GI + Olahraga 150 Min/Mgg → Target BB Turun 5-10%) + COC (Drospirenon 3 mg + EE 0,02 mg) → Siklus Teratur, Hirsutisme ↓, Jerawat ↓ + Metformin 500 mg 2×/Hari (Insulin Resistensi) → 6 Bln: BB -8 kg, BMI 28, Siklus 28-30 Hari, Hirsutisme Score ↓, Insulin Normal**.

## Definisi & Diagnosis (Rotterdam Criteria 2003 — Konsensus Internasional)

**PCOS = Sindrom Heterogen: Hiperandrogenisme + Disfungsi Ovulasi + Morfologi Ovarium Polikistik** — **2 dari 3 Kriteria (Eksklusi Etiologi Lain)**.

| Kriteria Rotterdam                                   | Definisi Operasional                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Oligo-Atau Anovulasi**                          | **Oligomenorea (< 8 Haid/Tahun) / Amenorea Sekunder / Siklus > 35 Hari / Anovulasi Terbukti (Progesteron Luteal < 3 ng/mL / USG Serial Tidak Ada Ovulasi)**                                                                                  |
| **2. Hiperandrogenisme (Klinis ATAU Biokimia)**      | **Klinis: Hirsutisme (Ferriman-Gallwey Score ≥ 8), Jerawat Vulgaris Berat, Alopecia Androgenetik (Female Pattern Hair Loss)** — **Biokimia: Testosteron Total ↑ / Free Testosteron ↑ / Androstenedion ↑ / DHEAS ↑ (FAI = T/SHBG × 100 > 5)** |
| **3. Morfologi Ovarium Polikistik (PCOM) — USG TVS** | **≥ 20 Folikel 2-9 mm PER Ovarium (FNPO ≥ 20) ATAU Volume Ovarium > 10 mL (Tanpa Kista Dominan > 10 mm) — **Khusus USG Modern (Transvaginal, Probe ≥ 8 MHz)\*\*                                                                              |

> **EKSKLUSI WAJIB SEBELUM DIAGNOSA PCOS:**
>
> - **Non-Classic CAH (21-Hydroxylase Deficiency): 17-OHP Basal > 2 ng/mL → ACTH Stim Test**
> - **Androgen-Secreting Tumor: Testosteron > 150-200 ng/dL / DHEAS > 700 μg/dL / Onset Cepat / Virilisasi → MRI/USG Adrenal/Ovarium**
> - **Hipertiroid / Hipotiroid: TSH, FT4**
> - **Hiperprolaktinemia: Prolaktin > 50 ng/mL → MRI Hipofisis**
> - **Sindrom Cushing: Dexamethasone Suppression Test / UFC 24 Jam**
> - **Akromegali: IGF-1**
> - **Obat (Androgen, Valproat, Sitotoksik, Danazol)**

## Fenotip PCOS (4 Fenotip Berdasarkan Rotterdam)

| Fenotip                     | Kriteria                                            | Prevalensi | Risiko Metabolik                                     |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **A (Klasik Lengkap)**      | **Oligoanovulasi + Hiperandrogenisme + PCOM**       | ~30%       | **Tinggi (IR, DM, Dislipidemia, Sindrom Metabolik)** |
| **B (Klasik Non-PCOM)**     | **Oligoanovulasi + Hiperandrogenisme (Tidak PCOM)** | ~20%       | **Tinggi**                                           |
| **C (Ovulatori)**           | **Hiperandrogenisme + PCOM (Ovulasi Normal)**       | ~30%       | **Sedang (IR Rendah vs A/B)**                        |
| **D (Non-Hiperandrogenik)** | **Oligoanovulasi + PCOM (Tidak Hiperandrogenisme)** | ~20%       | **Rendah (Kontroversial Apakah PCOS Benar)**         |

## Patogenesis (Multifaktorial)

| Mekanisme                                                      | Penjelasan                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Insulin Resistensi (IR) + Hiperinsulinemia Kompensatori**    | **Intrinsik (Genetik) + Ektrinsik (Obesitas) → Insulin Stimulasi Sel Theca → Androgen ↑ + LH Pulsatilitas ↑ + Hepatik SHBG ↓ → Free Testosteron ↑↑** |
| **Disfungsi Hipotalamus-Hipofisis (LH Pulsatilitas Cepat)**    | **GnRH Pulse Cepat → LH ↑↑, FSH Normal/Rendah → Ratio LH:FSH > 2-3**                                                                                 |
| **Genetik (Kompleks, Poligenik)**                              | **DENND1A, THADA, FSHR, LHCGR, INSR, FTO, DLL — Heritabilitas ~70%**                                                                                 |
| **Epigenetik & Lingkungan (Rahim, Anak Kecil, Obesitas, EDC)** | **Programming Janin → IR & Hiperandrogenisme Dewasa**                                                                                                |

## Gejala & Tanda (Spektrum Luas)

| Sistem                        | Manifestasi                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Reproduktif**               | **Oligomenorea, Amenorea, Infertilitas (Anovulasi #1), Aborsi Spontan ↑, Hiperplasia Endometrium ↑ (Unopposed Estrogen), Karsinoma Endometrium ↑ (3×)**                                                                                                      |
| **Kulit (Hiperandrogenisme)** | **Hirsutisme (FG Score ≥ 8), Jerawat Vulgaris (Mandibula/Leher/Dada), Alopecia Androgenetik (Ludwig Pattern), Acanthosis Nigrikans (Lipatan: Leher, Aksila, Leher Paha) — Tanda IR**                                                                         |
| **Metabolik**                 | **Obesitas Sentral (Perut) 40-80%, Insulin Resistensi (HOMA-IR > 2,5), Pre-DM/DM Tipe 2 (10-15% Usia < 40 Th), Dislipidemia (TG ↑, HDL ↓, LDL Kecil Tebal), Sindrom Metabolik (ATP III: 3 dari 5), Hipertensi, NAFLD (Lemak Hati Non-Alkohol), Apnea Tidur** |
| **Psikologis**                | **Depresi, Kecemasan, Body Image Buruk, Gangguan Makan, Kualitas Hidup ↓**                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Jangka Panjang**            | **DM Tipe 2, Kardiovaskular (Koroner, Stroke), Kanker Endometrium (3×), Apnea Tidur, NAFLD → Sirots**                                                                                                                                                        |

## Workup Diagnostik (Stepwise)

### 1. Anamnesis & Fisik (WAJIB)

- **Riwayat Menarche, Siklus, Haid, Hamil, Keluarga (DM, PCOS, Kardiovaskular)**
- **Hirsutisme: Ferriman-Gallwey Score (9 Area: Dahi, Leher Atas, Dada, Perut Atas, Perut Bawah, Punggung Atas, Punggung Bawah, Lengan Atas, Paha Atas) — 0-4 Tiap Area, Total ≥ 8 = Positif**
- **Alopecia: Ludwig Scale (I-III)**
- **Acanthosis Nigrikans: Inspeksi Lipatan**
- **BMI, Lingkar Perut (Sentral: Wanita > 80 cm), TD**

### 2. Laboratorium (Panel Minimum)

| Pemeriksaan                                                                                                                            | Cut-off / Interpretasi                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Testosteron Total (LC-MS/MS Preferred)**                                                                                             | **> 50-60 ng/dL (Tinggi) — Beda Lab, Gunakan Referensi Lab**                                                             |
| **Free Testosteron (Equilibrium Dialysis) / FAI (T/SHBG×100)**                                                                         | **Free T > 1,5-2% / FAI > 5 = Hiperandrogenisme Biokimia**                                                               |
| **Androstenedion, DHEAS**                                                                                                              | **Androstenedion ↑ Khusus Ovarium, DHEAS ↑ Khusus Adrenal**                                                              |
| **LH, FSH (Hari 2-5 Siklus / Random Jika Amenorea)**                                                                                   | **LH > 10 mIU/mL, Ratio LH:FSH > 2-3 (Spesifik Tapi Tidak Sensitif — Hanya 40% PCOS)**                                   |
| **AMH (Anti-Müllerian Hormone)**                                                                                                       | **> 4,5-5 ng/mL (Tinggi, Korelasi FNPO) — Bisa Bantuan Diagnosis Jika USG Tidak Tersedia**                               |
| **17-OHP (Basal, Hari 2-5)**                                                                                                           | **> 2 ng/mL → ACTH Stim (Non-Classic CAH)**                                                                              |
| **Prolaktin, TSH, FT4**                                                                                                                | **Rule Out Hiperprolaktinemia, Tiroid**                                                                                  |
| **Profil Metabolik: Glukosa Puasa, Insulin Puasa, HOMA-IR, HbA1c, OGTT 75g (2 Jam), Lipid Lengkap, Fungsi Hati (ALT, AST, GGT), eGFR** | **IR: HOMA-IR > 2,5 / Insulin Puasa > 12 μIU/mL / OGTT 2 Jam > 140 mg/dL (IGT) / > 200 (DM) — NAFLD: ALT > 30 (Wanita)** |
| **Vitamin D (25-OH D)**                                                                                                                | **Defisiensi Umum PCOS (< 20 ng/mL) — Suplemen Bisa Bantu IR & Ovulasi**                                                 |

### 3. USG Transvaginal (TVS) — **PCOM Assessment**

| Parameter                                                                                                       | Cut-off (USG Modern ≥ 8 MHz)                          |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **FNPO (Follicle Number Per Ovary) 2-9 mm**                                                                     | **≥ 20 PER Ovarium (Total FNPO ≥ 40 Jika Bilateral)** |
| **Volume Ovarium (0,5 × D1 × D2 × D3)**                                                                         | **> 10 mL (Tanpa Kista Dominan > 10 mm)**             |
| **Stroma: Hiperekoik, Tebal**                                                                                   | **Kualitatif**                                        |
| **Catatan:** **PCOM Bisa Normal Usia < 25 Th (FNPO Tinggi Fisiologis) → Gunakan Volume > 10 mL Lebih Spesifik** |

## Tata Laksana (Individualisasi Berdasarkan Fenotip & Tujuan Pasien)

### A. Lifestyle Modification (First-Line SEMUA PCOS — **Fondasi**)

| Target                                                                                                     | Evidensi                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **BB Turun 5-10% (Jika BMI > 25)**                                                                         | **Ovulasi Pulih 60-80%, IR ↓ 30-50%, Androgen ↓ 20-30%, Siklus Teratur, Hirsutisme ↓, Kualitas Hidup ↑** |
| **Diet: Low GI / Mediterranean / DASH / Low Carb Moderat (40-45% Karbo, 25-30% Protein, 30% Lemak Sehat)** | **Serat 25-30 g, Gula Tambahan < 25 g, Hindari Proses Tinggi, Makan Teratur**                            |
| **Olahraga: Aerobik 150 Menit/Mgg + Resistansi 2×/Mgg**                                                    | **Sensitivitas Insulin ↑, Visceral Fat ↓, Androgen ↓**                                                   |
| **Tidur 7-9 Jam, Manajemen Stres, Stop Rokok, Batasi Alkohol**                                             | **Kortisol & IR ↓**                                                                                      |

### B. Proteksi Endometrium (WAJIB Jika Oligomenorea/Amenorea > 3 Bln)

| Opsi                                                                                                          | Indikasi                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **COC (Combined Oral Contraceptive) — First-Line**                                                            | **Siklus Teratur, Hiperandrogenisme ↓ (SHBG ↑ → Free T ↓), Kontra, Proteksi Endometrium** — **Pilih: Drospirenon/Chlormadinon/Cyproteron (Anti-Androgenik) Jika Hirsutisme/Jerawat** |
| **Progestin Siklus (MPA 10 mg 10-14 Hari/Bln / Noretisteron 5-10 mg 10-14 Hari / Dienogest 2 mg 10-14 Hari)** | **Jika Kontraindikasi Estrogen (Rokok > 35 Th, Trombofilia, Migren Aura, HT Tidak Terkendali, Obesitas BMI > 35)**                                                                   |
| **LNG-IUS (Mirena)**                                                                                          | **Proteksi Endometrium Terbaik + Kontra + Kurang SE Sistemik — Tapi Tidak Atasi Hirsutisme/Jerawat (Lokal Saja)**                                                                    |
| **Metformin Saja (Tidak Cukup Proteksi Endometrium!)**                                                        | **HANYA Adjunct, BUKAN Pengganti Progestin/COC Untuk Proteksi Endometrium**                                                                                                          |

### C. Hiperandrogenisme (Hirsutisme, Jerawat, Alopecia)

| Terapi                                                                                                               | Detail                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **COC (Anti-Androgenik: Drospirenon, Chlormadinon, Cyproteron) — First-Line**                                        | **Free T ↓ 40-50%, Hirsutisme Score ↓ 30-50% (Butuh 6-9 Bln Efek Maksimal)**                                                                                              |
| **Anti-Androgen Tambahan (Jika COC 6-9 Bln Gagal / Kontraindikasi Estrogen)**                                        | **Spironolacton 50-100 mg/hari (Blokir Reseptor Androgen + Blokir Sintesa) — **Kontraindikasi: Hiperkalemia, Ginjal, Kehamilan (Kategori X) — Harus Pakai Kontrasepsi\*\* | **Flutamide 250 mg 2×/Hari (Hepatotoksisitas Risiko, Monitoring Liver) — Bupropion? Tidak** |
| **Topikal: Eflornithine 13,9% Krim (Vaniqa) — Wajah, 2×/Hari**                                                       | **Adjunct, Lambat Pertumbuhan Rambut, Tidak Menghilangkan**                                                                                                               |
| **Laser / IPL / Elektrolisis**                                                                                       | **Definitif Penghilangan Rambut — Butuh Banyak Sesi, Mahal, Paling Efektif Kombinasi Farmako + Laser**                                                                    |
| **Alopecia: Minoxidil 2-5% Topikal, Finasteride 2,5-5 mg/hari (Off-Label, Kontraindikasi Kehamilan), Spironolacton** | **Konsul Kulit**                                                                                                                                                          |

### D. Infertilitas (Anovulasi) — **Ovulasi Induksi**

| Line                                                                                    | Terapi                                                                                                                                                                       | Sukses Rate (Per Siklus / Kumulatif 6 Siklus) |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Letrozol (Aromatase Inhibitor) 2,5-7,5 mg Hari 3-7**                               | **First-Line Gold Standard (RCT: Letrozol > Klomifen Live Birth Rate)** — **Ovulasi 60-70%, Hamil 20-25%/Siklus, Live Birth 25-30% Kumulatif** — **Risiko Kembar < 5%**      |
| **2. Klomifen Sitrat (CC) 50-150 mg Hari 3-7**                                          | **Second-Line (Jika Letrozol Tidak Ada/Gagal)** — **Ovulasi 70-80%, Hamil 15-20%/Siklus, Live Birth 20-25% Kumulatif** — **Anti-Estrogenik Endometrium Tipis, Kembar 8-10%** |
| **3. Metformin (Adjunct) 1500-2000 mg/hari**                                            | **Tambahan Letrozol/CC Jika IR / Obesitas — Ovulasi & Live Birth ↑ Sedikit**                                                                                                 |
| **4. Gonadotropin (rFSH/hMG) — Low-Dose Step-Up Protocol**                              | **Gagal CC/Letrozol (CC-Resistant) — **Butuh Monitoring Ketat USG + E2 (Risiko OHSS & Kembar Tinggi)** — **Hanya Pusat IVF/SpOG Terampil\*\*                                 |
| **5. Laparoskopik Ovarian Drilling (LOD) — Diathermy/Laser 4-10 Punctures Per Ovarium** | **Gagal Gonadotropin / Tidak Bisa Monitoring Intensif — **Ovulasi 60-80%, Hamil 40-50% Kumulatif, Risiko Adhezi & Kadar Ovarium Turun\*\*                                    |
| **6. IVF / ICSI**                                                                       | **Gagal Semua Atas / Faktor Lain (Tuba, Sperma, Usia > 35) — **Live Birth Rate Tinggi, Risiko OHSS (Antagonist Protocol + Agonist Trigger + Freeze-All)\*\*                  |

### E. Insulin Resistensi & Sindrom Metabolik

| Terapi                                                                      | Indikasi & Evidensi                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Metformin 1500-2000 mg/hari (Titrasi 500 mg Mingguan Hindari GI SE)**     | **IR (HOMA-IR > 2,5), Pre-DM, DM Tipe 2, Obesitas, Adjunct Ovulasi Induksi, Hirsutisme (Sedikit Bantu)** — **Live Birth ↑ Jika Letrozol+Metformin vs Letrozol Saja (Subgroup Obesitas/IR)** |
| **Inositol (Myo-Inositol 2-4 g + D-Chiro Inositol 50-100 mg — Ratio 40:1)** | **Suplemen: IR ↓, Ovulasi ↑, Kualitas Oosit ↑, AMH Normalisasi — **Adjunct, Bukan Ganti Metformin/Letrozol\*\*                                                                              |
| **GLP-1 RA (Semaglutid/Liraglutid) — Jika Obesitas BMI > 30 + DM/Pre-DM**   | **BB Turun 10-15%, IR ↓ Drastis, Ovulasi Pulih Banyak Kasus — **Off-Label PCOS, Mahal, Butuh Monitoring\*\*                                                                                 |
| **Statin (Jika LDL > 130 / Risiko CVD Tinggi)**                             | **Kardiovaskular Proteksi**                                                                                                                                                                 |

| Parameter                                                                       | Interval                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Siklus, BB, BMI, Lingkar Perut, TD, Hirsutisme Score, Jerawat**               | **3-6 Bulanan**                        |
| **Glukosa Puasa / HbA1c / OGTT (Jika IR/Pre-DM/Obesitas)**                      | **Tahunan (Lebih Sering Jika Pre-DM)** |
| **Lipid Profil, Fungsi Hati (ALT/AST/GGT), eGFR**                               | **Tahunan**                            |
| **USG TVS (Endometrium Tebal, Ovarium) — Jika Tidak Pakai COC/Progestin Rutin** | **Tahunan (Proteksi Endometrium)**     |
| **Vitamin D, TSH, Prolaktin (Jika Awal Abnormal)**                              | **Tahunan**                            |
| **Screening Depresi/Kecemasan (PHQ-9/GAD-7)**                                   | **Tahunan**                            |
| **Kardiovaskular Risk Assessment (Framingham/SCORE)**                           | **Tahunan Usia > 40 Th**               |

## Ringkasan untuk Dokter Umum

1. **PCOS = Sindrom: 2 dari 3 Rotterdam (Oligoanovulasi + Hiperandrogenisme + PCOM USG) — Eksklusi CAH, Tumor, Tiroid, Prolaktin, Cushing WAJIB**
2. **Fenotip A & B (Klasik) = Risiko Metabolik Tinggi (IR, DM, Dislipidemia, Sindrom Metabolik, Kardiovaskular, Kanker Endometrium)**
3. **First-Line SEMUA: **Lifestyle (BB Turun 5-10%, Diet Low GI, Olahraga 150 Min/Mgg)** — Fondasi Semua Terapi**
4. **Proteksi Endometrium WAJIB (Jika > 3 Bln Tanpa Haid): **COC (First-Line) / Progestin Siklus / Mirena** — Metformin Saja TIDAK Cukup!**
5. **Hirsutisme/Jerawat: **COC Anti-Androgenik (Drospirenon/Cyproteron) First-Line → Spironolacton Tambahan (Harus Kontrasepsi!)** + Laser/Elektrolisis Definitif**
6. **Infertilitas Anovulasi: **Letrozol 2,5-7,5 mg Hari 3-7 = First-Line Gold Standard (Live Birth > Klomifen)** → Klomifen → Gonadotropin (Monitoring Ketat) → LOD → IVF**
7. **IR/Metabolik: **Metformin 1500-2000 mg Adjunct (IR, Obesitas, Pre-DM, Adjunct Ovulasi)** — Inositol Adjunct, GLP-1 RA Jika Obesitas Berat**
8. **Monitoring Jangka Panjang: **Metabolik (Glukosa, Lipid, Hati, TD) Tahunan, Endometrium USG Tahunan, Kardiovaskular, Psikologis, Vitamin D\*\*"
9. \*\*PCOS = Penyakit Sistemik Sepanjang Hayat — Bukan Hanya "Kista Ovarium" — Butuh Pendekatan Multidisiplin (SpOG, Endokrin, Kulit, Gizi, Psikolog, Bedah Bariatri Jika Obesitas Berat)"
