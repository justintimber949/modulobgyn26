# Kehamilan Ganda (Twin Pregnancy)

## Cerita Pembuka

Ny. Sari, 32 tahun, G2P1A0, kehamilan IVF (2 embrio transfer). USG 7 minggu: **Dikori Diami (DD)** — 2 kantung amnion, 2 plasenta (atau 1 plasenta fused tapi membran dikori), **membran tipis (T-sign)**. UK 20 mg: USG anatomik normal keduanya, EFW 300g & 310g, discordance 3%, cervix panjang 38 mm. ANC 4 mingguan USG pertumbuhan + Doppler UA. 32 mg: diskordansi 18% (EFW 1800g vs 1500g), Doppler UA bayi kecil AEDV. Betametason 12 mg 2× 24j. SC 33+2 mg: bayi 1850g & 1550g, NICU 3 minggu. Keduanya pulang sehat.

## Klasifikasi Korionalitas & Amnionalitas (WAJIB DITENTUKAN USG < 14 MG)

| Tipe                       | Korion | Amnion | Plasenta       | Membran                          | Risiko Utama                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------- | ------ | ------ | -------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dikori Diami (DD)**      | 2      | 2      | 2 (atau fused) | **Tipis (T-sign / Lambda sign)** | **Terendah** — tapi tetap risiko PTB, IUGR, preeklampsia                                                                |
| **Monokori Diami (MD)**    | 1      | 2      | 1              | **Tipis (T-sign)**               | **TTTS, TAPS, SIUGR, TRAP** — **monitoring ketat 2 mingguan**                                                           |
| **Monokori Monoamni (MM)** | 1      | 1      | 1              | **Tidak ada membran**            | **Tali pusat belit (cord entanglement), kongenital anomaly ↑, PTB sangat tinggi** — **inpatient 24-28 mg, SC 32-34 mg** |

> **Diagnosis Korionalitas USG < 14 mg (Ideal 11-13 mg):**
>
> - **Lambda Sign (Δ):** Plasenta fused tapi 2 korion → **DD** (spesifik 95%)
> - **T-Sign:** Membran datar ke plasenta → **MD** atau **DD fused tapi membran shared** — butuh cek jumlah plasenta & gender
> - **Tidak ada membran teraba** → **MM**
> - **Gender beda → PASTI DD** (Monokori selalu same gender)

## Epidemiologi & Faktor Risiko

| Faktor                             | Insidensi / Risiko                                    |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Spontan**                        | ~ 1:80 (1,25%) — variasi etnis (Yoruba 4%, Asia 0,5%) |
| **IVF / ART**                      | **20-30%** (tergantung jumlah embrio transfer)        |
| **Usia ≥ 35 th**                   | ↑ FSH → ovulasi ganda                                 |
| **Multipara**                      | ↑                                                     |
| **Riwayat keluarga (ibu/saudara)** | ↑ (genetika ovulasi ganda)                            |
| **BMI ≥ 30**                       | ↑                                                     |
| **Tinggi badan > 165 cm**          | ↑                                                     |

## Diagnosis & Penentuan Korionalitas (USG 11-13 Minggu)

| Parameter                     | DD                    | MD                | MM            |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------- | ------------- |
| **Jumlah Plasenta**           | 2 (bisa fused)        | 1                 | 1             |
| **Membran**                   | Tipis, **Lambda (Δ)** | Tipis, **T-sign** | **Tidak ada** |
| **Gender**                    | Bisa beda / sama      | **Sama**          | Sama          |
| **Kantung Kuning (Yolk Sac)** | 2                     | **1 (shared)**    | 1             |

> **Jika ragu USG 11-13 mg → Rujuk SpOG KFM / USG Detail** — manajemen beda drastis (MD butuh screening TTTS 2 mingguan, MM butuh rawat inap).

## Komplikasi Spesifik Kehamilan Ganda

### 1. Preterm Birth (PTB) — **Komplikasi #1**

- **Rata-rata lahir:** DD 36 mg, MD 35 mg, MM 32-33 mg
- **PTB < 37 mg:** DD 50%, MD 60%, MM > 90%
- **PTB < 34 mg:** DD 10%, MD 15%, MM 40%
- **Pencegahan:** Progesterone vaginal 200 mg malam **TIDAK efektif** untuk ganda (RCT negatif). **Serviks panjang (CL > 35 mm) = prediktor baik**. Sirkulase **tidak rutin** (hanya jika CL < 15-20 mg + riwayat PTB). Pessarium evidence lemah.

### 2. TTTS (Twin-Twin Transfusion Syndrome) — **Hanya Monokori (MD/MM)**

| Stadium Quintero | Kriteria                                                                                 | Tata Laksana                                            |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **I**            | Diskordansi volume (MVP donor < 2 cm, resipien > 8 cm) + bladder donor teraba            | **Observasi ketat USG 1-2 mingguan** (bisa regresi 30%) |
| **II**           | Stadium I + **bladder donor TIDAK teraba**                                               | **Laser Fetoskopik Selektif (SFLP) — Gold Standard**    |
| **III**          | Stadium II + **Doppler abnormal** (UA AEDV/REV donor / Vena dorsalis pulsatile resipien) | **SFLP SEGERA**                                         |
| **IV**           | Stadium III + **Hidrops resipien**                                                       | **SFLP DARURAT** (prognosis resipien buruk)             |
| **V**            | **IUFD satu bayi**                                                                       | Konseling, monitoring survivor (risiko neuro 15-20%)    |

> **Screening TTTS:** USG 2 mingguan **16-26 mg** (peak incidence) — cek MVP (Maximum Vertical Pocket), bladder, Doppler UA/DV, EFW diskordansi.

### 3. TAPS (Twin Anemia Polycythemia Sequence) — **Monokori**

- **Anastomosis arteriovenus kecil** → transfer darah lambat → **Donor: Anemia (Hb < 11 g/dL / MCA-PSV > 1,5 MoM), Resipien: Polisitemia (Hb > 18 g/dL / MCA-PSV < 0,8 MoM)**
- **Tanpa diskordansi volume** (bedanya dengan TTTS)
- **Diagnosis:** MCA-PSV Doppler 2 mingguan (sama TTTS screening)
- **Tata Laksana:** Expectant (Stadium I), Laser SFLP (Stadium II-IV), IUT/IPEX (resipien), Transfusi (donor)

### 4. sIUGR (Selective IUGR) — **Monokori (MD/MM)**

- **Diskordansi EFW > 20-25%** + **Doppler UA abnormal pada bayi kecil** (AEDV/REV)
- **Klasifikasi Gratacós:**
  - **Type I:** UA Doppler normal (placental share cukup) → expectant
  - **Type II:** UA AEDV/REV persist → **Laser SFLP** (jika < 28 mg) / **SC** (jika ≥ 28-30 mg)
  - **Type III:** UA Doppler fluktuatif (intermittent AEDV/REV) → unpredictable, monitoring ketat, SC individualized

### 5. TRAP (Twin Reversed Arterial Perfusion) — **Monokori Monoamni (atau MD dengan acardiac)**

- **Acardiac twin (parasite):** Tanpa jantung / jantung rudimenter, perfusi dari **Pump twin** via anastomosis arterio-arterial → **Pump twin risiko CHF, hidrops, IUFD**
- **Diagnosis:** USG: satu janin normal, satu acardic (no cardiac activity, perfusi retrograd UA)
- **Tata Laksana:** **RFA (Radiofrequency Ablation) / Bipolar Coagulation / Laser** ke pembuluh acardic → stop flow, selamatkan pump twin. Ideal **16-24 mg**.

### 6. IUFD Satu Bayi (Single Intrauterine Fetal Death)

| Korionalitas | Risiko Survivor                                                                           | Tata Laksana                                                                                                 |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **DD**       | Rendah (plasenta terpisah) → risiko preterm, DIC (jarang)                                 | Expectant, monitoring koagulasi mingguan, SC 37-38 mg                                                        |
| **MD/MM**    | **Tinggi (15-20% neurodisability, IUFD co-twin 10-15%)** — emboli trombotik & hemodinamik | **Konseling risiko neuro**, MRI otak 2 minggu pasca IUFD, SC 34-36 mg (balance prematuritas vs risiko neuro) |

## Tata Laksana Umum (Antenatal)

### USG Monitoring Schedule

| Korionalitas | USG Pertumbuhan + Doppler                                                   | Cervix Length (TV)            |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **DD**       | **4 mingguan** (20, 24, 28, 32, 36 mg)                                      | 18, 22, 28, 32 mg             |
| **MD**       | **2 mingguan** (16, 18, 20... sampai lahir) — **TTTS/TAPS/sIUGR screening** | 16, 18, 20, 22, 24, 28, 32 mg |
| **MM**       | **1-2 mingguan** + **Inpatient 24-28 mg** (cord entanglement monitoring)    | Inpatient continuous          |

### Screening & Pencegahan Lain

- **Preeklampsia:** Risiko 3-4× singleton → **Aspirin 150 mg malam 12-16 mg** (WAJIB)
- **Anemia:** Kebutuhan Fe 2× → **Suplementasi Fe 60-120 mg + Folat 1 mg + B12**
- **GDM:** Risiko ↑ → OGTT 24-28 mg (lebih awal jika risiko tinggi)
- **Nutrisi:** + 600 kkal/hari (300/bayi), Protein 1,5 g/kg, Kalsium 1500-2000 mg
- **Aktivitas:** Kurangi aktivitas berat > 24 mg, istirahat kiri lateral

## Timing & Mode Persalinan

| Korionalitas & Kondisi                        | Rekomendasi Timing                                       | Mode                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **DD, Dichorionic, Tidak Komplikasi**         | **37+0 - 38+6 mg** (ideal 37-38 mg)                      | **Vaginal jika Bayi 1 Cephalic** — Bayi 2: ECV internal / breech extraction / SC jika gagal / non-cephalic |
| **DD, Bayi 1 Non-cephalic**                   | 37-38 mg                                                 | **SC Elektif**                                                                                             |
| **MD, Tidak Komplikasi (No TTTS/TAPS/sIUGR)** | **36+0 - 37+6 mg** (ideal 36-37 mg)                      | Vaginal jika Bayi 1 Cephalic (sama DD)                                                                     |
| **MD, Post-Laser TTTS / sIUGR Type I**        | 36-37 mg                                                 | Sesuai presentasi                                                                                          |
| **MD, sIUGR Type II/III / TTTS Residual**     | **32-34 mg** (individualized)                            | Sering SC                                                                                                  |
| **MM (Monoamniotic)**                         | **32+0 - 34+6 mg** (ideal 32-33 mg)                      | **SC WAJIB** (cord entanglement risiko IUFD akut)                                                          |
| **Kehamilan Tiga (Triplet)**                  | **34-35 mg** (Triplet) / **32-33 mg** (Monokori triplet) | **SC WAJIB**                                                                                               |

> **Bayi 1 Cephalic + Bayi 2 Non-cephalic (DD/MD):**
>
> - Lahirkan Bayi 1 normal → **Internal Podalic Version + Breech Extraction** untuk Bayi 2 (operator senior, SC standby)
> - Atau **SC Elektif** dari awal (banyak SpOG pilih ini untuk safety)

## Persalinan Vaginal Kembar (Bayi 1 Cephalic)

| Tahap                          | Bayi 1                                                                                          | Bayi 2                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fase 1**                     | Monitoring CFM ganda (2 transduser / 1 transduser + fetal scalp electrode bayi 1)               | Monitoring terus                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Fase 2 (Pengejanan Bayi 1)** | Normal, hindari episio rutin                                                                    | Posisi: Tunggu 15-30 menit (kontraksi kembali)                                                                                                                       |
| **Interval**                   | Oksitosin augmentasi **WAJIB** (interval > 30-60 menit → risiko abrupsio, malpresentasi bayi 2) | **USG real-time** konfirmasi presentasi bayi 2                                                                                                                       |
| **Bayi 2**                     | Sudah lahir                                                                                     | **Cephalic:** Normal / Vacuum<br>**Bokong:** Internal version + Breech extraction (Mauriceau-Smellie-Veit)<br>**Samping:** Internal version → Breech extraction / SC |
| **Plasenta**                   | Tunggu plasenta 1 & 2 (bisa terpisah)                                                           | Oxytocin 20-40 IU IV infus pasca bayi 2                                                                                                                              |

> **Komplikasi Khusus Persalinan Kembar:**
>
> - **Abrupsio pasca bayi 1** (dekompresi uterus tiba-tiba) → Oksitosin cepat, SC standby
> - **Diskordansi besar** → Bayi 2 kecil risiko hipoglikemia, asfiksia
> - **Locked Twins** (Bayi 1 bokong, Bayi 2 cephalic, dagu bayi 1 terkunci di bawah dagu bayi 2) → **SC Darurat** (tidak bisa vaginal)

## Pasca Persalinan & Neonatal

| Bayi                     | Risiko Khusus                                       | Monitoring                                   |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Keduanya**             | Prematuritas (RDS, IVH, NEC, Sepsis, PDA, ROP)      | NICU level 2/3 sesuai UK & BB                |
| **Donor (TTTS/TAPS)**    | Anemia, Hipovolemia, IUGR                           | Transfusi, nutrisi parenteral                |
| **Resipien (TTTS/TAPS)** | Polisitemia, Hiperviskositas, Kardiomegali, Hidrops | Partial exchange transfusion, diuretik, echo |
| **MM**                   | Cord entanglement sequelae                          | Neurodevelopment follow-up ketat             |

## Ringkasan untuk Dokter Umum

1. **USG 11-13 mg WAJIB tentukan Korionalitas (DD/MD/MM)** — manajemen beda drastis
2. **DD: USG 4 mingguan. MD: USG 2 mingguan (TTTS/TAPS/sIUGR). MM: Inpatient 24-28 mg, SC 32-34 mg**
3. **Aspirin 150 mg 12-16 mg WAJIB** (preeklampsia risiko 3-4×)
4. **TTTS Screening 2 mingguan 16-26 mg** (MVP, Bladder, Doppler UA/DV/MCA)
5. **Timing Persalinan:** DD 37-38 mg, MD 36-37 mg, MM 32-34 mg (SC wajib MM)
6. **Vaginal Bisa JIKA Bayi 1 Cephalic** — Bayi 2: internal version/breech extraction, SC standby
7. **IUFD Satu Bayi:** DD expectant, MD/MM → konseling neuro risiko, MRI, SC 34-36 mg
8. **Rujuk SpOG KFM SEGERA** jika: MD/MM, TTTS, sIUGR, TRAP, diskordansi > 20%, anomali
