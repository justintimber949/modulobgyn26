# Karsinoma Endometrium (Endometrial Cancer)

## Cerita Pembuka

Ny. Sari, 56 tahun, G2P2A0, menopause 6 tahun, BMI 34 (Obesitas), DM Tipe 2 (HbA1c 7,8%), HT. Keluhan: **PMB (Postmenopause Bleeding) seitan 2 bln: spotting merah segar, tidak nyeri**. USG TVS: **Endometrium Tebal 14 mm, Heterogen, Vaskularitas Warna Tinggi (Color Doppler)**. Pipelle Biopsy: **Endometrioid Adenocarcinoma Grade 2**. MRI Panggul: **Tumor Mencapai > 50% Miometrium (Stage IB), Serviks Tidak Tembus, Adneksa Normal, Limfa Pelvik Tidak Besar**. CT Thoraks/Abdomen: Normal. Operasi: **Histerektomi Total + BSO + Pelvic Lymph Node Dissection (PLND) + Paraaortic Lymph Node Sampling (PALN) + Omentektomi + Peritoneal Biopsy + Pelvic Washing — Laparoskopik**. Histopat Final: **Endometrioid Adenocarcinoma Grade 2, Invasi Miometrium 60% (pT1b), LVSI Negatif, Limfa Negatif (0/22 Pelvik, 0/8 Paraaortik) → pT1bN0M0 → FIGO 2009 Stage IB / FIGO 2023 Stage 1B**. Adjuvant: **Vaginal Brachytherapy (VBT) 3×5 Gy (Porter) — Tidak Kemo (Grade 2, LVSI-, N0, < 60 Th)**. 3 Th: NED.

## Epidemiologi & Faktor Risiko

| Parameter | Data |
|-----------|------|
| **Insidensi Global** | **Kanker Ginekologi #1 Negara Maju, #2-3 Negara Berkembang (Stelah Serviks)** — ~417.000 Kasus/Tahun, ~97.000 Meninggal |
| **Indonesia** | **Meningkat Drastis (Obesitas, DM, Usia Harapan Hidup Naik)** — Perkiraan 5.000-7.000 Kasus/Tahun |
| **Usia Puncak** | **60-70 Th (Postmenopause)** — 75% > 50 Th, < 5% < 40 Th |
| **Faktor Risiko Utama (Unopposed Estrogen)** | **Obesitas (BMI > 30) #1 — Risiko 3-10× (Aromatase Perifer), DM Tipe 2 (IR + Hiperinsulinemia), HT, PCOS (Anovulasi Kronik), Nullipara, Menopause Lambat (> 55), Estrogen Only HT (Tanpa Progestin), Tamoksifen (Agonis Endometrium), Sindrom Lynch (HNPCC) — Genetik 3-5% (MSH2, MLH1, MSH6, PMS2, EPCAM)** |
| **Protektif** | **COC (Kurangi 50% Risiko 20 Th Pasca Stop), Multipara, Menyusui, Activitas Fisik, BB Normal, Progestin (Mirena, MPA)** |

## Patogenesis & Klasifikasi Molekuler (TCGA 2013 / FIGO 2023)

**Dua Jalur Utama (Bokhman):**
1. **Tipe I (Endometrioid, Estrogen-Driven, Low Grade) — 80%:** Hiperplasia → EIN → CA Endometrioid G1/G2 — **Mutasi: PTEN, PIK3CA, KRAS, CTNNB1 (Beta-Catenin), MMR-d (MSI-H), ARID1A** — **Prognosis Baik**
2. **Tipe II (Non-Endometrioid, Estrogen-Independent, High Grade) — 20%:** Serous, Clear Cell, Carcinosarcoma, Undifferentiated — **Mutasi: TP53 (Serous), PPP2R1A, PIK3CA (Clear Cell) — Prognosis Buruk**

### Klasifikasi Molekuler TCGA (Prognostik & Terapi Terarah) — **WHO 2020 / FIGO 2023 Adopsi**
| Subtipe Molekuler | Frekuensi | Histologi Umum | Prognosis | Terapi Implikasi |
|-------------------|-----------|----------------|-----------|------------------|
| **POLE Ultramutated (POLEmut)** | 5-10% | Endometrioid G3 (Sering) | **Sangat Baik (Recurrence < 5%)** | **De-escalation: Tidak Perlu Adjuvant RT/Kemo (Jika Early Stage)** |
| **MMR-d / MSI-H (Mismatch Repair Deficient)** | 25-30% | Endometrioid G1-3 | **Baik-Sedang** | **Immunoterapi (Pembrolizumab/Dostarlimab) Jika Lanjut/Residiv — Lynch Screening WAJIB** |
| **p53 Abnormal (p53abn)** | 15-20% | Serous, Clear Cell, Carcinosarcoma, Endometrioid G3 | **Buruk (Agresif, Recurrence Tinggi)** | **Agresif: Kemo + RT + Target (PARPi Trial)** |
| **NSMP (No Specific Molecular Profile)** | 40-50% | Endometrioid G1-2 | **Intermediate** | **Standar Berdasarkan Klinikopatologi (Grade, Stage, LVSI)** |

> **Praktik: Semua Karsinoma Endometrium Harus Dites Molekuler (IHC: p53, MMR Proteins MSH2/6/MLH1/PMS2, POLE Sequencing Jika Tersedia) — Memandu Adjuvant & Lynch Screening.**

## Histologi (WHO 2020)

| Tipe | Frekuensi | Grade | Perilaku |
|------|-----------|-------|----------|
| **Endometrioid Adenocarcinoma** | **75-80%** | G1 (≤ 5% Solid), G2 (6-50% Solid), G3 (> 50% Solid) | **Tipe I (Kecuali G3 Bisa Tipe II Jika Molekuler p53abn)** |
| **Serous Carcinoma** | 10% | High Grade (Selalu G3) | **Tipe II (Agresif, Early Metastasis, p53abn)** |
| **Clear Cell Carcinoma** | 5% | High Grade | **Tipe II (Agresif, Glikogen Clear Cytoplasm, p53 Wildtype, PIK3CA Mut)** |
| **Carcinosarcoma (MMMT - Malignant Mixed Müllerian Tumor)** | 3-5% | High Grade (Epitel + Mesankimal) | **Tipe II (Sangat Agresif)** |
| **Undifferentiated / Dedifferentiated** | < 1% | High Grade | **Tipe II (Terburuk)** |
| **Mesenchymal (Sarkoma Uteri: Leiomyosarcoma, Endometrial Stromal Sarcoma, Undifferentiated Uterine Sarcoma)** | 3-5% | Grade Berbeda | **Bukan Karsinoma — Lihat Modul Sarcoma** |

## Gejala & Tanda

| Gejala | Frekuensi |
|--------|-----------|
| **Postmenopause Bleeding (PMB) — **Patognomonik, Harus Dicek Semua PMB**** | **90%+** |
| **Abnormal Uterine Bleeding (AUB) Perimenopause: Menorrhagia, Metrorrhagia, Spotting** | 10% (Usia Muda) |
| **Pelvic Pain / Massa / Tekanan (Stadium Lanjut)** | Stadium III-IV |
| **Gejala Sistemik: BB Turun, Anoreksia, Fatigue (Stadium IV)** | Stadium IV |

## Diagnosis & Workup

### 1. Screening & Diagnosis Awal
| Pemeriksaan | Indikasi & Cut-off |
|-------------|-------------------|
| **USG TVS (Transvaginal) — First-Line** | **ET (Endometrial Thickness) > 4 mm (Postmenopause PMB) → Biopsi WAJIB** — **ET ≤ 4 mm: NPV 99%+ (Bisa Hentikan Jika Asimtomatik, Tapi PMB Tetap Biopsi)** |
| **Pipelle Biopsy / Tao Brush / Z-Sampler** | **First-Line Biopsi (Sensitivit 80-90%) — Jika Negatif Tapi PMB Persisten / ET > 10 mm / Heterogen → Histeroskopi** |
| **Histeroskopi + Biopsi Terarah (D&C Di Bawah Penglihatan)** | **Gold Standard (Sensitivitas 95-99%) — Visualisasi Lesi, Polip, Hiperplasia, CA — WAJIB Jika Pipelle Negatif Tapi Klinis Curiga** |

### 2. Staging Workup (Pre-Operatif)
| Modality | Indikasi |
|----------|----------|
| **MRI Panggul (Kontras Gadolinium) — Gold Standard Lokal** | **Kedalaman Invasi Miometrium (< 50% / > 50%), Invasi Serviks (Stromal), Adneksa, Limfa Pelvik/Paraaortik (Size > 1 cm / Morfologi), Vagina, Bladder/Rectum** |
| **CT Thoraks/Abdomen/Pelvis** | **Staging Jauh (Paru, Hati, Limfa Toraks/Abdomen, Tulang) — Stadium ≥ IB / Grade 3 / Non-Endometrioid / LVSI+ / Limfa Besar MRI** |
| **PET-CT (FDG)** | **Lebih Sensitif Limfa & Metastasis Jauh vs CT — Preferred Jika Tersedia (Khusus Non-Endometrioid / Lanjut)** |
| **CA-125, HE4, ROMA** | **Monitoring & Staging Tambahan (CA-125 ↑ Korelasi Stage & Burden)** |
| **Koloskopi / Kistoskopi** | **Jika Gejala GI/GU / Stadium Lanjut (IVA/IVB) / MRI Curiga Invasi Bladder/Rectum** |

### 3. Patologi & Molekuler (WAJIB Semua Kasus)
- **Histologi + Grade (WHO 2020)**
- **LVSI (Lymphovascular Space Invasion): Ya/Tidak — Penting Adjuvant**
- **Molekuler (IHC Minimum): p53 (Normal/Abnormal), MMR (MSH2, MSH6, MLH1, PMS2) — Retensi/Hilang**
- **POLE Sequencing (Jika Tersedia / Endometrioid G3 / MMR-d / p53wt)**
- **Lynch Screening: MMR-d / MSI-H → Germline Testing (MLH1 Promoter Methylation Dulu Rule Out Sporadik) → Konseling Genetik**

## Staging (FIGO 2009 vs FIGO 2023) — **FIGO 2023 Baru (Molekuler Terintegrasi)**

| Stage FIGO 2009 | Definisi | FIGO 2023 (Ringkas) |
|-----------------|----------|---------------------|
| **IA** | Tumor Terbatas Endometrium / Invasi < 50% Miometrium | **Stage 1A** (Sama) |
| **IB** | Invasi ≥ 50% Miometrium | **Stage 1B** (Sama) |
| **II** | Invasi Stromal Serviks (Tidak Hanya Glandular) | **Stage 2** (Sama) |
| **IIIA** | Invasi Serosa Uteri / Adneksa (Ovarium/Tuba) / Positive Peritoneal Washing | **Stage 3A** (Serosa/Adneksa) / **Stage 1A/1B/2/3A** (Peritoneal Washing Dihapus Dari Staging) |
| **IIIB** | Metastasis Vagina / Parametrium | **Stage 3B** (Vagina) / **Stage 3C** (Parametrium) |
| **IIIC1** | Metastasis Limfa Pelvik | **Stage 3C1** (Pelvik) |
| **IIIC2** | Metastasis Limfa Paraaortik (± Pelvik) | **Stage 3C2** (Paraaortik) |
| **IVA** | Invasi Mukosa Bladder / Rectum | **Stage 4A** (Sama) |
| **IVB** | Metastasis Jauh (Paru, Hati, Tulang, Limfa Inguinal, Peritoneal Jauh) | **Stage 4B** (Sama) |

> **FIGO 2023 Tambahan: Integrasi Molekuler (POLEmut, MMRd, p53abn, NSMP) Sebagai "Molecular Classification" Terpisah Dari Anatomic Stage — Memandu Adjuvant.**

## Tata Laksana (Multidisiplin — Tumor Board)

### A. Operasi (Standar Kuratif Stadium I-III) — **Laparoskopik Preferred (Feasible, Morbiditas Rendah)**
| Komponen Operasi | Standar |
|------------------|---------|
| **Histerektomi Total (Uterus + Serviks)** | **WAJIB** |
| **BSO (Bilateral Salpingo-Oophorectomy)** | **WAJIB (Kecuali Stadium IA G1 Non-EIN Molekuler POLEmut/MMRd/NSMP Usia < 45 Th Ingin Fertilitas → Opsi Ovarium Sparing Konsul Onkologi)** |
| **Pelvic Lymph Node Dissection (PLND) / Sentinel Lymph Node (SLN) Mapping** | **SLN Mapping (ICG/Technetium/Blue Dye) — Preferred (Morbiditas Rendah, Deteksi Ultra-Staging) — Jika SLN Negatif & Low Risk (IA G1-2, LVSI-) Bisa Skip PLND Lengkap** — **PLND Lengkap (Common Iliac, External Iliac, Internal Iliac, Obturator) Jika High Risk (Grade 3, LVSI+, > 50% Miometrium, Non-Endometrioid, Limfa Besar Pre-Op)** |
| **Paraaortic Lymph Node Sampling / Dissection (PALN)** | **Jika PLND Positif / High Risk (Non-Endometrioid, Grade 3, LVSI+, > 50% Miometrium, Tumor > 2 cm) — Sampling: Renal Vein Hingga IMA** |
| **Omentektomi (Infracolic / Total)** | **Non-Endometrioid (Serous, Clear Cell, Carcinosarcoma) / Endometrioid G3 / LVSI+ / Suspicious Omentum** |
| **Peritoneal Biopsy (Random / Suspicious)** | **Rutin** |
| **Pelvic Washing (Sitologi Peritoneal)** | **Rutin (Tapi Tidak Masuk Staging FIGO 2023)** |
| **Apendektomi** | **Serous / Clear Cell / Carcinosarcoma / Mucinous / Endometrioid G3 / LVSI+** |

> **Fertility-Sparing (Stadium IA G1 Endometrioid, Non-EIN, Ingin Anak):** **Progestin Intensif (LNG-IUS + MPA 200-400 mg/hari / Depot MPA 400 mg IM 3 Bulanan) + Histeroskopi Biopsi 3 Bulanan → Resolusi → Cepat Hamil (IVF) → Pasca Melahirkan Histerektomi** — **Konsul Onkologi, Genetik, Reproduksi.**

### B. Terapi Adjuvant (Pasca Operasi Berdasarkan Stage + Histologi + Molekuler + LVSI)

#### **Stage IA (Endometrioid G1-G2, LVSI-) — Low Risk**
| Molekuler | Adjuvant |
|-----------|----------|
| **POLEmut** | **Tidak Perlu Adjuvant (Observasi Saja)** |
| **MMRd / NSMP / p53abn** | **Observasi (VBT Opsional Jika Faktor Risiko: Usia > 60, LVSI Fokal, Grade 2)** |

#### **Stage IB (Endometrioid G1-G2, LVSI-) — Intermediate Risk**
| Molekuler | Adjuvant |
|-----------|----------|
| **POLEmut** | **Observasi** |
| **MMRd / NSMP** | **VBT (Vaginal Brachytherapy) 3-4 Fraksi × 5-7 Gy (Total 15-21 Gy) — PORTEC-2: VBT = EBRT Untuk Kontrol Vaginal, SE Lebih Rendah** |
| **p53abn** | **EBRT (External Beam RT) 45-50.4 Gy + VBT Boost (Agresif)** |

#### **Stage I-II (Endometrioid G3 / LVSI+) — High-Intermediate Risk**
| Molekuler | Adjuvant |
|-----------|----------|
| **POLEmut** | **VBT (Observasi Opsi)** |
| **MMRd / NSMP** | **EBRT 45-50.4 Gy ± VBT Boost (PORTEC-3: EBRT + Kemo Cisplatin vs EBRT Saja — EBRT+Kemo Lebih Baik DFS Tapi OS Sama, SE Lebih Tinggi)** |
| **p53abn** | **EBRT + Kemo (Carboplat AUC5-6 + Paklitaksel 175 mg/m2 × 6 Siklus) — Agresif** |

#### **Stage III-IV (Lanjut) / Non-Endometrioid (Serous, Clear Cell, Carcinosarcoma)**
| Situasi | Adjuvant |
|---------|----------|
| **Stage III (Lokal Lanjut)** | **EBRT 45-50.4 Gy + Kemo (Carbo/Pakli × 6) — PORTEC-3 / GOG-258: ChemoRT > RT Saja** |
| **Stage IV (Metastatik) / Recurrence** | **Kemo Sisteminik (Carbo/Pakli × 6) ± Immunoterapi (Pembrolizumab/Dostarlimab Jika MMRd/MSI-H — KEYNOTE-158, GARNET) ± Target (Lenvatinib+Pembrolizumab — KEYNOTE-775) ± PARPi (Jika HRD/BRCA)** |
| **Serous / Clear Cell / Carcinosarcoma (Early Stage IA-IB)** | **Kemo (Carbo/Pakli × 3-6) ± VBT/EBRT (Agresif Karena High Grade)** |

### C. Terapi Lanjut/Metastatik (Stage IV / Recurrence)
| Line | Regimen |
|------|---------|
| **First-Line** | **Carboplat AUC5-6 + Paklitaksel 175 mg/m2 × 6 (Standar) — **Jika MMRd/MSI-H: Immunoterapi (Pembrolizumab 200 mg 3 Mingguan / Dostarlimab 500 mg 3 Mingguan) Bisa First-Line (Off-Label/Compassionate) ATAU Maintenance Pasca Kemo** |
| **Second-Line+** | **Dostarlimab/Pembrolizumab (Jika MMRd/MSI-H Belum Dapat) — Lenvatinib 20 mg + Pembrolizumab 200 mg (KEYNOTE-775, Semua Komer, OS ↑) — Doksorubisin Liposomal / Paklitaksel Weekly / Topotekan / Gemsitabin — PARPi (Olaparib/Niraparib) Jika HRD/BRCA Mutasi — Klinis Trial** |
| **Hormonal (Endometrioid Low Grade, ER+ Recurrence)** | **Progestin (MPA 200-400 mg/hari / LNG-IUS / Megestrol 160 mg/hari) / AI (Letrozol/Anastrozol) / Tamoksifen — Response Rate 20-30%** |

## Follow-up (Pasca Terapi Kuratif)

| Waktu | Evaluasi |
|-------|----------|
| **Setiap 3-4 Bulan (Th 1-2)** | **Anamnesis (PMB, Nyeri, BB, GI/GU), Pemeriksaan (Spekulum, Bimanual, Rektal, Limfa Inguinal), CA-125 (Opsional, Bisa Monitoring), USG TVS (Vault, Adneksa, Limfa)** |
| **Setiap 6 Bulan (Th 3-5)** | **Sama + CT Thoraks/Abdomen/Pelvis Tahunan (Opsional, Jika High Risk / Gejala)** |
| **Tahunan (Th > 5)** | **Klinis + CA-125 + USG TVS + CT Tahunan (Selekitf)** |
| **Lynch Syndrome (MMRd Germline)** | **Koloskopi 1-2 Tahunan, USG Ginjal, Endoskopi Atas, Kulit, Ginekologi Tahunan (Ovarium/Endometrium/Serviks Risiko ↑)** |

## Ringkasan untuk Dokter Umum

1. **Kanker Endometrium = Kanker Ginekologi #1 Negara Maju, Meningkat di Indonesia (Obesitas, DM, Usia) — PMB (Postmenopause Bleeding) = Gejala #1 (90%) → **SEMUA PMB HARUS WORKUP (USG TVS ET > 4 mm → Biopsi)****"
2. **Diagnosis: USG TVS ET > 4 mm (Postmenopause) → Pipelle Biopsy → Histeroskopi (Gold Standard) → Molekuler (p53, MMR, POLE) WAJIB"
3. **Klasifikasi Molekuler TCGA (POLEmut, MMRd, p53abn, NSMP) = **Kunci Prognosis & Adjuvant** — POLEmut = Baik Banget (De-escalation), p53abn = Buruk (Agresif)"
4. **Operasi Standar: Histerektomi Total + BSO + SLN Mapping (ICG) ± PLND/PALN ± Omentektomi ± Apendektomi (Sesuai Risiko) — Laparoskopik Preferred"
5. **Adjuvant Berbasis Risiko: Stage IA G1-2 LVSI- = Observasi (POLEmut) / VBT (Lain) | Stage IB G1-2 LVSI- = VBT | G3/LVSI+ = EBRT ± Kemo | Stage III = ChemoRT | Stage IV/Non-Endometrioid = Kemo Sisteminik ± Immuno/Target"
6. **Lynch Syndrome: MMRd/MSI-H → MLH1 Methylation Rule Out → Germline Testing → Konseling Genetik → Screening Keluarga & Koloskopi 1-2 Tahunan"
7. **Follow-up: Klinis + USG TVS + CA-125 3-4 Bulanan (2 Th) → 6 Bulanan (3-5 Th) → Tahunan — CT Selektif"
8. **Pencegahan: **Kontrol BB (Obesitas #1), COC (Protektif 20 Th), Progestin/Mirena (Anovulasi/HT), Screening PMB, Metformin (IR/DM), Lifestyle**""
