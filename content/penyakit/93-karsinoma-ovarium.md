# Karsinoma Ovarium (Ovarian Cancer)

## Cerita Pembuka

Ny. Lestari, 54 tahun, G3P3A0, menopause 4 tahun. Keluhan: **perut kembung (bloating) seitan 4 bln, naik BB 3 kg tapi nafsu makan turun, nyeri perut bawah samar, BAK sering**. Pemeriksaan: **Abdomen Distensi, Cairan Asites (+), Massa Adneksa Kanan Teraba 10×8 cm, Konsistensi Keras, Tidak Bergerak, Limfa Inguinal Tidak Teraba**. Lab: **CA-125 1.245 U/mL (Tinggi), HE4 850 pmol/L, ROMA 85% (High Risk), Hb 10,5 (Anemia), Albumin 3,2**. USG TVS: **Massa Kompleks Kanan 12×9×8 cm, Septasi, Papilari, Vaskularitas Tinggi, Cairan Asites Besar**. CT Thoraks/Abdomen/Pelvis: **Massa Ovarium Kanan, Implant Peritoneal Omentum & Serosa Usus, Limfa Pelvik & Paraaortik > 1 cm, Pleural Effusion Kanan Kecil → cT3cN1M0 (Stage IIIC)**. MDT: **Primary Debulking Surgery (PDS) Feasible (CT-Based Score < 8) → PDS: Histerektomi Total + BSO + Omentektomi + Pelvic/Paraaortic LND + Peritonectomy + Diaphragm Stripping + Appendektomi + Splenektomi Parsial + Cholecystectomy → R0 (No Residual)**. Histopat: **High-Grade Serous Carcinoma (HGSC) Grade 3, Bilateral, Positive Pelvic (3/24) & Paraaortic (2/12) Nodes, Omentum+, Peritoneal+, pT3cN1M0 → Stage IIIC**. BRCA Test: **BRCA1 Mutasi (Germline)**. Adjuvant: **Carboplat AUC5 + Paklitaksel 175 mg/m2 × 6 + Bevacizumab 15 mg/kg (Siklus 2-6) → Maintenance Olaparib 300 mg 2×/Hari 2 Tahun (SOLO-1)**. 18 Bln: NED, CA-125 Normal.

## Epidemiologi & Faktor Risiko

| Parameter | Data |
|-----------|------|
| **Insidensi Global** | **Kanker Ginekologi #2 (Stelah Endometrium/Korpus) — Tapi Meninggal #1 Ginekologi (Diagnosis Sering Lanjut)** — ~314.000 Kasus/Tahun, ~207.000 Meninggal |
| **Indonesia** | **Kanker Ginekologi #2 — Perkiraan 8.000-10.000 Kasus/Tahun — 70% Stadium III-IV Saat Diagnosis** |
| **Usia Puncak** | **55-65 Th (Postmenopause)** — 90% Epitelial, 10% Non-Epitelial (Germ Cell, Sex Cord-Stromal) |
| **Faktor Risiko** | **Nullipara, Infertilitas, Endometriosis, Menarche Dini, Menopause Lambat, Estrogen Only HT, Obesitas, Rokok (Mucinous), Talc (Kontroversial), Asbes** |
| **Protektif** | **COC (Kurangi 50% Risiko 20-30 Th Pasca Stop), Multipara, Menyusui, Ligasi Tuba / Salpingektomi (Kurangi 30-50%), Histerektomi (Tanpa BSO) (Kurangi 30%)** |
| **Genetik (Hereditary 15-25%)** | **BRCA1 (40-50% Hereditari), BRCA2 (20-30%), Lynch (MMR: MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, PMS2), RAD51C/D, BRIP1, PALB2 — **HGSC & Endometrioid/Clear Cell Paling Sering BRCA/Lynch** |

## Patogenesis & Sel Asal (Dualistic Model)

| Model | Tipe Histologi | Sel Asal | Mutasi Kunci | Perilaku |
|-------|----------------|----------|--------------|----------|
| **Tipe I (Low-Grade, Stepwise)** | **Low-Grade Serous (LGSC), Endometrioid, Clear Cell, Mucinous, Borderline (BOT)** | **Ovarium Surface Epithelium (OSE) / Inklusi Kista / Endometriosis (Endometrioid/Clear Cell)** | **KRAS, BRAF, NRAS (LGSC/BOT), CTNNB1 (Beta-Catenin, Endometrioid), ARID1A, PIK3CA (Clear Cell/Endometrioid), KRAS (Mucinous)** | **Lambat, Stadium Awal Sering, Kemo Kurang Responsif (Kecuali Clear Cell)** |
| **Tipe II (High-Grade, De Novo)** | **High-Grade Serous (HGSC) — 70% Epitelial, Carcinosarcoma, Undifferentiated** | **Fimbria Tuba Fallopi (STIC - Serous Tubal Intraepithelial Carcinoma) → Implant ke Ovarium/Peritoneal** | **TP53 (96-100%), BRCA1/2 (Germline/Somatic 20-25%), HRD (Homologous Recombination Deficiency 50%), CCNE1 Amplifikasi** | **Agresif, Cepat Metastasis Transkoelik, Stadium Lanjut Saat Diagnosis, Responsif Kemo (Platinum), PARP Inhibitor Target** |

> **Konsep Penting: **Salpingektomi (Bilateral) = Pencegahan HGSC (Tipe II) — Direkomendasikan Pada Histerektomi Benign / Ligasi Tuba / Risiko Tinggi (BRCA) — **Opportunistic Salpingectomy**.**

| **Clear Cell** | 10% | High (G3) | **ARID1A, PIK3CA, TP53 (Jarang), HNF1B** | **Tromboembolism Risiko Tinggi, Resisten Platinum, Asosiasi Endometriosis** |
| **Mucinous** | 3-5% | G1-G3 | **KRAS, CDKN2A, TP53 (G3), HER2 (Amplifikasi Subset)** | **Besar, Unilateral, Harus Rule Out Metastasis GI (Krukenberg) / Appendix / Pankreas** |

| **Borderline (BOT)** | 10-15% Semua Epitelial | Non-Invasif (Atipia Tapi Tidak Invasi Stromal) | **KRAS, BRAF (Serous), KRAS (Mucinous)** | **Usia Muda, Stadium IA > 70%, Fertility-Sparing Bisa, Recurrence Non-Invasif/Invasif** |

## Gejala & Tanda (Vague — "Silent Killer")

| Gejala (Serinng > 12×/Bln, Baru, Persisten) | Frekuensi |
|---------------------------------------------|-----------|
| **Bloating / Distensi Perut (Early Satiety / Penuh Cepat)** | **70%+** |
| **Nyeri Pelvik / Perut Bawah** | 50% |
| **Gejala Uriner: Frekuensi, Urgensi, Nokturia** | 30% |
| **Perubahan Kebiasaan BAK / Sembelit** | 20% |
| **BB Turun Tidak Disengaja / Anoreksia / Fatigue** | Stadium Lanjut |
| **Asites (Perut Besar Cepat, Nafsu Makan Turun)** | Stadium III-IV |
| **Massa Adneksa Teraba (Unilateral/Bilateral, Keras, Tidak Bergerak, Nodular)** | Pemeriksaan Fisik |

> **Indeks Gejala (Goff Index): Bloating + Nyeri Pelvik + Early Satiety + Urgensi BAK — ≥ 1 Gejala > 12×/Bln & < 1 Tahun Durasi → Sensitivitas 57%, Spesifisitas 87% Untuk Deteksi Awal.**

## Diagnosis & Workup

### 1. Screening (Tidak Direkomendasikan Populasi Umum — PLCO & UKCTOCS: Tidak Turunkan Mortalitas)
| Kelompok | Rekomendasi |
|----------|-------------|
| **Risiko Rata-Rata** | **TIDAK SCREENING Rutin (CA-125 + USG TVS) — False Positive Tinggi, Biopsi/Operasi Tidak Perlu, Tidak Turunkan Mortalitas** |
| **Risiko Tinggi (BRCA1/2, Lynch, Riwayat Keluarga Kuat)** | **CA-125 + USG TVS 6-12 Bulanan (Mulai Usia 30-35 Th / 5-10 Th Sebelum Usia Keluarga Diagnosis) — **Bukan Screening Standar, Opsi Konseling Genetik & Risk-Reducing Surgery (RRSO: BSO Usia 35-40 Th BRCA1, 40-45 Th BRCA2) Lebih Efektif**" |

### 2. Diagnosis Awal (Kasus Klinis Curiga)
| Pemeriksaan | Peran |
|-------------|-------|
| **CA-125 (Serum)** | **Marker Epitelial (Tidak Spesifik: Naik Juga Endometriosis, PID, Asites, Haid, Kehamilan, Mioma, Liver, Peritonitis) — **Tinggi > 35 U/mL = Curiga, > 200 U/mL + Massa Adneksa = Sangat Curiga Malign** — **HE4 + ROMA (Risk of Ovarian Malignancy Algorithm) Lebih Spesifik Premenopause** |
| **USG TVS (Transvaginal) + Doppler** | **First-Line Imaging: Masa Adneksa (Unilateral/Bilateral), Septasi, Papilari, Solid Component, Vaskularitas Warna (Color Score 1-4), Asites, Limfa, Omental Caking** — **IOTA Simple Rules / ADNEX Model / O-RADS (Ovarian-Adnexal Reporting and Data System) — **O-RADS 4-5 = High Risk → Rujuk Onkologi** |
| **CT Thoraks/Abdomen/Pelvis (Kontras) ATAU PET-CT** | **Staging (T, N, M) — **Gold Standard Pre-Op** — **Asites, Peritoneal Implant, Omental Caking, Limfa (> 1 cm), Pleural Effusion, Parenchymal Metastasis** |
| **MRI Panggul (Jika CT Kontraindikasi / Detail Adneksa)** | **Karakterisasi Massa (T1/T2/DWI), Rule Out Endometrioma vs Malignan** |
| **Biomarker Tambahan** | **CEA, CA 19-9 (Rule Out Mucinous Metastasis GI), AFP, β-hCG, LDH (GCT), Inhibin (SCST)** |

### 3. Diagnosis Patologi Definitif — **HANYA DARI JARINGAN OPERASI (TIDAK BIOPSI PERKUTAN PRE-OP JIKA PDS Direncanakan!)**
| Prinsip | Alasan |
|---------|--------|
| **Tidak Biopsi Perkutan (FNAB/Core) Jika PDS (Primary Debulking Surgery) Direncanakan** | **Risiko Tumor Seeding (Port Site/Peritoneal) — Upstaging → Stage IC1 (Intraoperative Rupture) vs IC3 (Pre-Op Rupture/Biopsy) — **Biopsy HANYA Jika Neoadjuvant (NACT) Direncanakan / Tidak Bisa Operasi / Diagnostik Paliatif** |
| **Frozen Section Intraoperatif** | **Konfirmasi Malign vs Benign / Borderline → Menentukan Extent Operasi (Fertility-Sparing vs Radical)** |
| **Histopat Final + IHC + Molekuler** | **WHO 2020 Klasifikasi, BRCA/HRD Testing (Germline + Somatic), MMR IHC, HRD Score (Myriad/MyChoice/FoundationOne)** |

## Staging (FIGO 2014 — Masih Digunakan, FIGO 2023 Update Minor)

| Stage | Definisi |
|-------|----------|
| **IA** | Tumor Terbatas 1 Ovarium (Kapsul Intak, Tidak Ada Tumor Di Permukaan, Tidak Ada Sel Malign Di Asites/Peritoneal Washing) |
| **IB** | Tumor Terbatas 2 Ovarium (Kapsul Intak, Tidak Ada Tumor Di Permukaan, Tidak Ada Sel Malign Di Asites/Peritoneal Washing) |
| **IC** | **Tumor Terbatas Ovarium (1/2) DENGAN: IC1: Ruptura Intraoperatif (Spillage), IC2: Kapsul Robek Sebelum Operasi / Tumor Di Permukaan Ovarium, IC3: Sel Malign Di Asites / Peritoneal Washing** |
| **II** | **Ekstensi Pelvik: IIA: Uterus/Tuba, IIB: Organ Pelvik Lain (Bladder/Rectum/Sigmoid), IIC: + Sel Malign Asites/Peritoneal Washing** |
| **III** | **Implant Peritoneal Di Luar Pelvik (Abdomen): IIIA: Mikroskopik (Hanya Mikroskopik Di Peritoneal / Omentum / Limfa Retroperitoneal Hanya Mikro), IIIB: Makroskopik ≤ 2 cm (Peritoneal / Omentum), IIIC: Makroskopik > 2 cm (Peritoneal / Omentum) ATAU Limfa Retroperitoneal Positif (Pelvik/Paraaortik)** |
| **IV** | **Metastasis Jauh: IVA: Pleural Effusion (Positif Sitologi), IVB: Metastasis Parenkim (Paru, Hati, Limfa Inguinal, Tulang, Otak)** |

## Tata Laksana (MDT — Multidisciplinary Team / Tumor Board)

### A. Strategi Operasi vs Neoadjuvant (NACT) — **Keputusan Kunci Awal**

| Kriteria | Primary Debulking Surgery (PDS) | Neoadjuvant Chemo (NACT) + Interval Debulking Surgery (IDS) |
|----------|--------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Stage IIIC-IV (Bulan)** | **Jika Resectable to R0 (No Gross Residual) — Prediksi: CT-Based Score (Fagotti, Aletti, PCI) < 8 / Laparoskopi Eksploratif "Laparoscopy First" → Jika Bisa R0 → PDS** | **Jika Unresectable R0 (Bulan Banyak, Mesentery Root, Porta Hepatis, Diaphragm Luas, Parenchymal, Performance Status Buruk) — **NACT 3-4 Siklus Carbo/Pakli → Re-evaluasi CT → IDS → Adjuvant Kemo Sisa (Total 6-8 Siklus)** |
| **Stage I-II (Early)** | **PDS = Standar (Staging Lengkap + Debulking)** | **Tidak Direkomendasikan (Kurang Staging, Kurang Outcome)** |
| **Evidence** | **GOG-218, ICON7, CHORUS, SCORPION, TRUST, EORTC 55971 — **PDS R0 = OS Terbaik, Tapi Jika R0 Tidak Bisa → NACT Non-Inferior / Lebih Baik (Less Morbidity)** — **EORTC 55971 & CHORUS: NACT Non-Inferior OS, Lebih Sedikit Komplikasi** | |

> **Praktik: **Laparoskopi Eksploratif (Diagnostik + Assess Resectability) Sebelum Laparotomi PDS** — Jika R0 Bisa → PDS, Jika Tidak → Tutup, NACT 3-4 Siklus → IDS."

### B. Operasi Standar (PDS / IDS) — **Cytoreduction Tujuan: R0 (No Gross Residual) > R1 (< 1 cm) > R2 (> 1 cm)**
| Prosedur | Indikasi |
|----------|----------|
| **Histerektomi Total + BSO** | **WAJIB Semua Epitelial (Kecuali Fertility-Sparing Stadium IA/IC1 G1/BOT Usia Muda Ingin Anak — Lihat Bawah)** |
| **Omentektomi (Infracolic / Supracolic / Total)** | **WAJIB (Omentum #1 Situs Metastasis)** |
| **Pelvic & Paraaortic Lymph Node Dissection (PLND + PALN)** | **WAJIB Staging (Kecuali Stadium IA G1/BOT Fertility-Sparing) — **Sampling vs Dissection: Dissection Lebih Akurat, Sampling Cepat — SLN Mapping (ICG) Belum Standar Ovarium** |
| **Peritonectomy (Pelvic, Parietal, Diaphragmatic, Mesenteric, Serosal)** | **Sesuai Distribusi Tumor (R0 Target)** |
| **Rectosigmoid Resection + Anastomosis (Stapler/Hand-Sewn)** | **Jika Implant Rectosigmoid / Mesentery Root Involvement — **Diverting Stoma (Loop Ileostomi) Opsional Jika Anastomosis Risiko Tinggi / Radiasi Sebelumnya** |
| **Diaphragm Stripping / Resection (Pleural Effusion / Liver Surface)** | **Jika Implant Diaphragm / Liver Capsule** |
| **Splenektomi (Total / Parsial) + Distal Pancreatektomi (Jika Perlu)** | **Jika Implant Splenik / Pancreas Tail — **Vaksinasi Pre-Op (Pneumokok, Haemophilus, Meningokok) WAJIB** |
| **Cholecystectomy** | **Rutin Jika Omentektomi Supracolic / Portal Hepatis Dissection (Mudah, Mencegah Batu Emas Pasca Kemo)** |
| **Appendektomi** | **WAJIB (Rule Out Appendiceal Mucinous Neoplasm / Metastasis Mucinous Ovarium / Primary Appendiceal)** |
| **HIPEC (Hyperthermic Intraperitoneal Chemotherapy) — *KONTROVERSIAL*** | **OVHIPEC Trial (NACT + IDS + HIPEC Cisplatin 100 mg/m2 41°C 90 Min vs NACT+IDS Saja) — **OS ↑ (Median 45.7 vs 33.9 Bln), DFS ↑ — **HANYA PUSAT TERAMPIL, IDS + HIPEC, BUKAN PDS** — **Belum Standar Global (NCCN: Category 2B)** |

### C. Fertility-Sparing Surgery (FSS) — **Khusus Stadium IA/IC1 G1 (Endometrioid/Clear Cell/LGSC/Mucinous) & BOT Usia < 40 Th Ingin Anak**
| Kondisi | Prosedur FSS |
|---------|--------------|
| **Stadium IA/IC1 G1 Endometrioid/Clear Cell/LGSC/Mucinous** | **Unilateral Salpingo-Oophorectomy (USO) Sisi Tumor + Staging Lengkap (Omentektomi, LND, Peritoneal Biopsy, Appendektomi) — **Kontrolateral Ovarium + Uterus Dipertahankan** |
| **Borderline (BOT) Serous/Mucinous Stadium IA/IC1** | **USO (Serous) / Cystectomy (Mucinous) + Staging — **Uterus & Kontrolateral Ovarium Dipertahankan** |
| **Germ Cell Tumor (Dysgerminoma/Yolk Sac/Immature Teratoma) Stadium IA/IC1** | **USO + Staging + Kemo BEP (3-4 Siklus) — **Fertilitas Terjaga** |
| **Pasca FSS: Monitoring Ketat (CA-125, USG, MRI 3-6 Bln) → Pasca Selesai Keturunan → Completion Surgery (BSO + Histerektomi)** | |

### D. Terapi Adjuvant (Pasca PDS/IDS)

#### **Epithelial (HGSC, Endometrioid, Clear Cell, Mucinous, Carcinosarcoma)**
| Stage | Regimen Standar |
|-------|-----------------|
| **IA/IC1 G1 (Low Risk)** | **Observasi (Tidak Perlu Kemo) — **Kecuali Clear Cell (Pertimbangkan Kemo Carbo/Pakli × 3-6)** |
| **IA/IC1 G2-G3 / IB/IC / II** | **Carboplat AUC5-6 + Paklitaksel 175 mg/m2 × 6 Siklus (Setiap 3 Minggu) — **Dose-Dense Weekly Paklitaksel 80 mg/m2 (JGOG-3016: OS ↑ Jika Suboptimal Debulking) — **Bevacizumab 15 mg/kg (Siklus 2-6) Opsional (GOG-218, ICON7: PFS ↑, OS Tidak Signifikan Kecuali High Risk)** |
| **Stage III-IV (Optimal R0/R1 Debulking)** | **Carbo/Pakli × 6 + Bevacizumab (Siklus 2-6) → Maintenance Bevacizumab 15 mg/kg 15 Bln (GOG-218) / Olaparib (Jika BRCA/HRD) — **SOLO-1: Olaparib Maintenance 2 Th → PFS ↑ Drastis (HR 0,30) — **PAOLA-1: Olaparib + Bevacizumab Maintenance (BRCA/HRD) → PFS ↑** |
| **Stage III-IV (Suboptimal R2 / NACT+IDS)** | **Carbo/Pakli × 6-8 (Total) + Bevacizumab → Maintenance Olaparib (Jika BRCA/HRD) / Bevacizumab / Niraparib (PRIMA: Niraparib Maintenance Semua Komer Stage III-IV Respons Platinum, HRD ↑ Benefit)** |

#### **PARP Inhibitor Maintenance (Game Changer HGSC BRCA/HRD)**
| Obat | Indikasi | Trial Kunci |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **Olaparib** | **Germline/Somatic BRCA Mutasi (SOLO-1: First-Line Maintenance 2 Th) / HRD+ (PAOLA-1: + Bevacizumab)** | **SOLO-1 (BRCA), PAOLA-1 (HRD+Bev)** |
| **Niraparib** | **Semua Stage III-IV Respons Platinum (PRIMA: HRD ↑ Benefit, HRP Juga Benefit)** | **PRIMA, NORA** |
| **Rucaparib** | **BRCA Mutasi (ARIEL3/4) / HRD** | **ARIEL3** |

#### **Non-Epitelial**
| Tipe | Regimen |
|------|---------|
| **Germ Cell Tumor (GCT)** | **BEP (Bleomycin 30 U D1,8,15 + Etoposid 100 mg/m2 D1-5 + Cisplatin 20 mg/m2 D1-5) × 3-4 Siklus — **Dysgerminoma: Carbo/Pakli Bisa Alternatif (Kurang Bleomycin Toxicity)** |
| **Sex Cord-Stromal (Granulosa)** | **Observasi Stadium IA / Carbo/Pakli × 6 Stadium Lanjut / Hormonal (AI/Tamoksifen/Progestin) Recurrence** |

| **Clear Cell** | **Carbo/Pakli × 6 (Resisten Platinum) → Pertimbangkan Irinotekan+Sisplatin / Pembrolizumab (PD-1) / Lenvatinib+Pembrolizumab (Off-Label) / Klinis Trial** |

## Follow-up (Pasca Terapi Kuratif)

| Waktu | Evaluasi |
|-------|----------|
| **Setiap 3 Bulan (Th 1-2)** | **CA-125 (Kenaikan 2× Dari Nadir / > 35 U/mL = Recurrence Early Signal — **Rustin Criteria**), Pemeriksaan Klinis (Pelvik, Abdomen, Limfa, Payudara), USG TVS / CT (Selekitf Jika CA-125 Naik / Gejala)** |
| **Setiap 4-6 Bulan (Th 3-5)** | **Sama** |
| **Tahunan (Th > 5)** | **Klinis + CA-125 + USG/CT Tahunan** |
| **BRCA/HRD/Lynch Carrier** | **Payudara: MRI Tahunan (Usia 25-30 Mulai), Koloskopi 1-2 Tahunan (Lynch), Endometrium Screening, Prostate (Pria), Pankreas (BRCA2)** |

## Recurrence (Kambuh) — **Platinum-Sensitive vs Resistant**
| Kategori | Definisi | Terapi |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Platinum-Sensitive** | **Recurrence > 6 Bln Pasca Platinum Terakhir** | **Re-challenge Platinum (Carbo/Pakli / Carbo/Gem / Carbo/Pegylated Liposomal Doksorubisin) + Bevacizumab → Maintenance PARPi (Jika Belum / Bisa Ulang)** |
| **Platinum-Resistant** | **Recurrence < 6 Bln / Progres Saat Platinum** | **Non-Platinum: Pegylated Liposomal Doksorubisin (PLD) / Paklitaksel Weekly / Topotekan / Gemcitabin / Etoposid Oral / Bevacizumab + Non-Platinum (AURELIA: Bev+Chemo > Chemo Saja) / Pembrolizumab (Jika MSI-H/MMRd) / Lenvatinib+Pembrolizumab / Klinis Trial** |
| **Platinum-Refractory** | **Progres Selama Platinum** | **Sama Resistant + Best Supportive Care / Hospice Jika Opsi Habis** |

## Ringkasan untuk Dokter Umum

1. **Kanker Ovarium = "Silent Killer" — Gejala Vague (Bloating, Nyeri Pelvik, Early Satiety, Urgensi BAK) Baru, Persisten > 12×/Bln → Curiga!**
2. **Screening Populasi Umum: **TIDAK Direkomendasikan (CA-125 + USG Tidak Turunkan Mortalitas, False Positive Tinggi)** — Risiko Tinggi (BRCA/Lynch): CA-125+USG 6-12 Bln + **RRSO (BSO) Usia 35-45 Th Lebih Efektif**"
3. **Diagnosis: CA-125 + HE4 + ROMA + USG TVS (O-RADS/IOTA) + CT Thoraks/Abdomen/Pelvis (Staging) — **Biopsy Perkutan HANYA Jika NACT Direncanakan / Tidak Bisa Operasi**"
4. **Strategi: **PDS (R0 Target) vs NACT+IDS** — Laparoskopi Eksploratif Assess Resectability Dulu — **R0 = Kunci Survival**"
5. **Operasi: **Histerektomi + BSO + Omentektomi + PLND/PALN + Peritonectomy + Appendektomi + Cholecystectomy ± Bowel/Diaphragm/Splenectomy (Sesuai Tumor) — R0 > R1 > R2**"
6. **Adjuvant: **Carbo/Pakli × 6 + Bevacizumab (Opsional) → Maintenance PARPi (Olaparib/Niraparib) Jika BRCA/HRD (SOLO-1, PAOLA-1, PRIMA) — **Game Changer HGSC**"
7. **Non-Epitelial: **GCT → BEP (Fertility-Sparing Bisa), SCST → Hormonal/Kemo, LGSC → Hormonal/MEKi, Clear Cell → Resisten Platinum, Klinis Trial**"
8. **Follow-up: **CA-125 3 Bulanan (Rustin Criteria: 2× Nadir = Recurrence) + Klinis + Imaging Selektif**"
9. **Recurrence: **Platinum-Sensitive (> 6 Bln) → Re-challenge Platinum + Bev + PARPi Maintenance | Platinum-Resistant (< 6 Bln) → Non-Platinum + Bev + Immuno/Target/Klinis Trial**"
10. **Pencegahan: **COC (50% ↓ Risiko), Multipara, Menyusui, **Salpingektomi Bilateral (Opportunistic Saat Histerektomi/Ligasi Tuba) — Pencegahan HGSC (Tipe II) #1**, RRSO BRCA (BSO 35-45 Th)"
